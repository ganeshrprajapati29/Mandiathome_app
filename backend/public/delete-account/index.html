How to delete your account

Option 1 – in the app: open the Mandi At Home app → Account → Delete account → confirm.

Option 2 – on this page: enter the mobile number you use to log in, verify it with the OTP we send by SMS, and confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Can't access your number? Email support@mandiathome.com from the email on your account, or call support, with your registered mobile number. We complete such requests within 7 days after verifying that the account is yours.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, mobile number, email
  • All saved delivery addresses and map locations
  • Your wishlist and cart
  • Login and OTP records

What is kept, and for how long

  • Past order records (items, amounts, dates, city and pincode) are kept for up to 8 years, as Indian tax and accounting law requires. Your name, phone number and street address are removed from them.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. You can sign up again later with the same number as a new customer.
